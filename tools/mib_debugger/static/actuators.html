A move is refused while the clamp is lifted (calibration), and when a limit switch is active in that direction. Every move first reads the controller's clamp back; a controller that has power-cycled holds its factory [0, 0] and is re-initialised before the target goes out. The MCP266 firmware tested honours the velocity and ignores the acceleration fields (the ramp comes from Motion Studio's stored Accel/Decel). Too slow a velocity makes the loop cog.

Travel limits

min max
The MCP266 reverts to its EEPROM at power-up. Until saved, the limits are re-written at the next move or Initialize.
To calibrate a joint: lift the clamp, jog to one mechanical end, press ← current on min, jog to the other end, ← current on max, untick the clamp, then Write to controller. The clamp is Basicmicro's MinPos/MaxPos in the position PID record; 0x607D is the CiA 402 software limit. Both are written, and a target outside them is forced to the edge by the controller even if something bypasses this page.

Encoder count

set to
Writes the controller's count for this channel (command 22/23) with the loop taken down first, so it does not chase its old target. For an incremental encoder this is how the count becomes meaningful after power-up: drive to a known end and install the count that end should read. Read back and checked against the tolerance.
