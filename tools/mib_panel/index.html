    Flash firmware

    Over the air, the way the PACE RACER dashboard flashes a wheel: the board makes itself safe, writes its other app slot and reboots into it, and the panel reconnects and marks the new image valid. The MIB goes over the link you are connected with; a PACE RACER goes over Ethernet through eth_bridge.py.

    The target's firmware. A new image boots pending verify; an image that is never confirmed loses to the previous one at the next reset.

    Once, over USB: a MIB still on the old partition table (one factory app, no rollback) has nowhere to write an update. Flash this firmware with idf.py flash over the USB-C one last time; it writes the bootloader, the two-slot table and the app. A PACE RACER's first ethernet-capable image also goes over USB (tools/provision.py).
